## Q1: For Foreign Investors: What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》第180条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Financial Compliance] For Overseas Chinese: How is property divided in cross-border divorce?

**A**: Cross-border divorce property division involves multiple legal systems: 1) Determine jurisdiction court first; 2) Chinese property follows Chinese law, overseas property may follow local law; 3) Different countries have different standards for marital property; 4) Prenuptial agreements recommended.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国民法典》婚姻家庭编

**领域**: 婚姻家事 | **语言**: English
